AI or by hand? You decide.

You can build the site completely by hand, the way it's always been done, or with the help of the AI helpers. Both are equally good. The class decides, and nobody has to use AI.

AI is also used today where people have limitations. Anyone who can't use their hands for a keyboard controls the computer with their voice. Care homes are already trying out and using AI: speaking instead of typing for documentation, sensors that report a fall, and robot seals meant to calm people with dementia. The results are mixed, and experts say: AI doesn't replace care, it can only help.

If you like, try AI as a tool. If you don't, build by hand. You stay the authors: the AI only makes suggestions, and the committee approves.

Your profile or portrait

Plan: this is how it is meant to be. This feature doesn't exist yet; this is how it should look one day.

Anyone can be described by someone else. There are two forms: the profile with fixed fields, which contains only positive things, and the portrait, a short, friendly text of at most 120 words.

  1. Write. You write about someone in the class and can upload a photo with it.
  2. Check. Anything mean is stopped right away and not saved.
  3. The person sees it. The person you described reads it first, all on their own, and may improve it.
  4. Approval. The person described decides. If they're under 16, their parents have to agree too.

The person described always has the final say. Without their yes, nothing appears: the text stays invisible and is deleted after 30 days. And they can take their yes back at any time.

Important: photos and consent

Photos and names only go on the site if the person has clearly agreed themselves. Anyone under 16 also needs a yes from their parents. If you don't want to be on it, you stay out, and any photo can be deleted at any time.

On GitHub's free plan everything is public: anyone on the internet can see the site and all the pictures. That's why every photo in which someone can be recognised needs the consent of the person and their parents.

Kids write about kids

Either as a profile with fixed fields (first name, favourite food, favourite subject, hobby, “What I like about him/her”, “What he/she is especially good at”, one sentence about the party; no fields for weaknesses) or as a portrait of at most 120 words.

How it works

  • The child who is writing chooses whom they describe.
  • While writing, the check runs; anything not allowed is blocked immediately and not saved.
  • The text goes as a draft to the child described, who sees it first on their own and can improve it, reject it or approve it.
  • For under-16s the parents' consent is also needed. Only after both approvals is the text visible.
  • Without approval it stays invisible and is deleted after 30 days. Withdrawing consent deletes it immediately.
  • Feedback always goes through the child described, never through third parties.

What gets blocked

Swear words and insults in all spellings, attacks, mockery, put-downs, bullying, threats, humiliation, rumours, sexual content, violence, drugs, alcohol, nicotine and anything not cleared for under-18s, references to health, origin, religion, family and appearance, and nastiness wrapped up in friendly words.

How it is checked

  1. Word lists and patterns (German, Swiss German, youth slang, spelling variants).
  2. An AI moderation for content, tone and attacks.
  3. Fields that steer towards positive things.
  4. The veto of the child described.
  5. The report to the committee or the teacher, who can block it at any time.

No check catches everything, so this rule applies: nothing appears without the approval of the child described.

Data protection and photos

Only first names or initials, no surnames, no photos without the person's own approval, no details about health, origin, religion or family. The site is public, so: only with the consent of the child and the parents. Deletion after the graduation party, at the latest after the school year, immediately if consent is withdrawn.

Why we offer the project with AI support

Building and maintaining a site like this is tedious, and the work is spread over many months. Big projects like this often fail not because of the idea but because nobody enjoys doing the work any more. The AI takes the grind off the team so that the fun of the project stays.

The AI decides nothing. It suggests wording; approval and publication are decided by the child described and the committee. The children remain the authors, and photos and names only go on the site with the consent of the child and the parents. Building by hand is just as valid, and nobody has to use AI.
